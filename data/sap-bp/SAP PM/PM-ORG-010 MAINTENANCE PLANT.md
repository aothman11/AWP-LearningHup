## Definition 

A Maintenance Plant is a plant (site) in which the Technical Objects (functional locations, equipment) are installed and maintained. A plant is defined as a Maintenance Plant only when it is assigned to a Maintenance Planning Plant.

A Maintenance Plant can be responsible for its own planning, in which case it will be assigned to itself as Maintenance Planning Plant (i.e., it is both a Maintenance Planning Plant and a Maintenance Plant).

A Maintenance Plant can only be assigned to one Maintenance Planning Plant.

The Maintenance Plant can have further divisions like:
