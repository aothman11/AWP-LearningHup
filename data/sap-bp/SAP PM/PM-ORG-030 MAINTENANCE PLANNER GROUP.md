## Definition 

A Maintenance Planner Group is a 3-character predefined code, that is Groups of employees responsible for planning and processing maintenance tasks.* *

A Maintenance Planner Group can be assigned to only one plant. A Maintenance Planner Group can be one person. 

Maintenance Planner Group is useful for the selection of data (either Functional Location or Equipment). It can also be used for reporting purposes.
