## Definition 

A Maintenance Planning Plant is a plant in which maintenance tasks are prepared, planned, and scheduled. Material Planning is carried out on the basis of bills of material in task lists and orders.

Maintenance Planning Plants are selected from the list of logistics plants.

Maintenance notifications are created & maintenance orders are managed.
