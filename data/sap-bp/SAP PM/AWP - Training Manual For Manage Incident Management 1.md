                            Incidents

                            An incident is an unplanned, which means it is an undesired event that adversely affects
                            the completion of a task and/or endangers the safety of an employee or the
                            environment.

                            Near Misses

                            A near miss is an unplanned, which means it is an undesired event where no damage was
                            caused and no personal injury sustained, but where a slight shift in time or position,
                            damage and/or injury could have occurred easily.

                            Safety Observations

                            A safety observation is the identification and documentation of a potential inappropriate
                            or unsafe behavior or hazardous condition that could lead to an incident. A safety
                            observation could, for example, result from the following: planned or unplanned direct
                            observations employee suggests audits inspection job hazard analyses.
