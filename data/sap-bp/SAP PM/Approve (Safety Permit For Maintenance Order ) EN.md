1 Introduction

In this tutorial, we explain the new procedure on how to make an automated safety permit for a maintenance
order in the SAP system.


My goal is to do this:
   1. Ensuring the implementation of safety requirements
      Linking the maintenance order to the safety permit ensures that the work is carried out only after all the required safety
      requirements and approvals are met.
   2. Risk control during implementation
      helps to identify risks associated with maintenance work (electricity, hot works, elevations...), and ensure that preventive
      measures are taken before starting work.
   3. Enhanced adherence to procedures and regulations
      prevents any maintenance work outside the system or without an approved permit, which reduces accidents and violations.
   4. Track actions and responsibilities
      clarify who is authorized, who executes, and when to implement, which promotes accountability and transparency.
   5. The link between safety and operation
      integrates safety management and maintenance, making safety an essential part of the maintenance order lifecycle.
   6. Documentation and review
      provides an approved record that can be referenced in the event of an accident investigation or review of safety procedures.
   7. Reporting support and continuous improvement
      enables the analysis of the work associated with safety permits and the identification of high-risk activities to improve
      procedures in the future.

Practical Integration with SAP PM/Safety Permit
The SAP PM maintenance order is linked to the safety permit in order to ensure that no maintenance work that needs to be taken
and the safety specialist's authorization and supervision is not carried out until the approved safety requirements are met, through
the following steps:
   •   Create a maintenance request/maintenance order in SAP PM.
   •   Determining that the type of work requires a safety permit Working at heights - Confined spaces - Boiler maintenance works
       - Electrical maint.above380volts - Work in Explosive Atmospheres - Treatment plant maint.works - Cutting and welding -
       Excavation works
   •   Issue a safety permit from the safety team and link it to the maintenance order number.
   •   Initiation of execution or confirmation of the order (TECO/Confirmation) shall not be allowed unless there is a valid safety
       clearance.
   •   Documenting the permit data within the maintenance order for follow-up and audit purposes.
