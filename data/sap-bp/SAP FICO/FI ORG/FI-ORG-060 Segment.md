## Assignment of SAP Organizational Units (System Configuration)

Segments are assigned to each profit center created in the system and segments are derived to P&L from cost centers and profit centers. Segments inherited to balance sheets accounts from the opposite P&L entry. In case of debit and credit side are both balance sheet accounts, mandatory profit center field required to be entered.
