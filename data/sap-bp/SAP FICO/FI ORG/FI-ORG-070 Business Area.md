## Definition 

A business area is an organizational unit within accounting that represents a separate area of operations or responsibilities in a business organization.

Organizational unit of external accounting that corresponds to a specific business segment or area of responsibility in a company. Movements in value entered in Financial Accounting are assigned to business areas. Business Areas in SAP are used to differentiate transactions originating from different points/lines/locations in business.
